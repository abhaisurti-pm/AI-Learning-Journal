### Key AI PM Insight

Generative AI produces new content based on patterns learned from data and the input/context provided to the AI system.

AI-generated output should not automatically be treated as truth. AI projects require appropriate evaluation, controls, human oversight, and risk management based on the use case.

### Key AI PM Insight

An LLM is a technology component rather than a complete AI product.

The AI PM must determine whether an LLM is appropriate for the business problem and evaluate:

* Accuracy
* Cost
* Latency
* Scale
* Context requirements
* Security
* Privacy
* Reliability
* Business risk
* Human oversight
* Evaluation requirements

A fluent AI response is not necessarily a correct response.

**Fluent Output ≠ Guaranteed Correct Output**

The largest or most powerful model is also not automatically the best model. Model selection should be based on business requirements and technical constraints.
